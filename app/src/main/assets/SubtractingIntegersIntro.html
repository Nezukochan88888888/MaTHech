Subtracting Integers

Subtracting integers is the process of finding the difference between two integers. The result may either increase or decrease the value, depending on whether the integers involved are positive, negative, or a mix of both.

📋 Rules for Subtracting Integers

Rule 1: If you subtract 0 from any integer, the result is the integer itself.
Example: 7 − 0 = 7
Rule 2: If you subtract any integer from 0, the result is the additive inverse (opposite) of that integer.
Example: 0 − 5 = −5
Rule 3: Subtraction is performed by changing the sign of the subtrahend and proceeding to addition.
    Also performed by (KCC) Keep Change Change
  • Keep the first number.
  • Change the subtraction sign to addition.
  • Change the the sign of the subtrahend (the second number).
