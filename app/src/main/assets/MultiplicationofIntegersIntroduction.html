Multiplication of Integers

Multiplication of integers is the same as repetitive addition, which means adding an integer a specific number of times. Understanding the sign rules makes multiplication straightforward and predictable!

💡 What is Multiplication?

Multiplication is a shortcut for repeated addition. Instead of writing the same number multiple times, we can multiply!

3 × 4 means: 3 + 3 + 3 + 3 = 12

📋 Three Cases of Integer Multiplication

➕✖️➕
Positive × Positive
Multiplying two positive numbers always gives a positive result
➖✖️➖
Negative × Negative
Multiplying two negative numbers always gives a positive result
➕✖️➖
Mixed Signs
Multiplying numbers with different signs always gives a negative result

Sign Rule Memory Aid

+ × + = +
Same signs → Positive
− × − = +
Same signs → Positive
+ × − = −
Different signs → Negative
