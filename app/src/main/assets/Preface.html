✨ Why MaTHech?

The MaTHech application was developed to strengthen students' understanding of integers through interactive and enjoyable learning experiences. Inspired by the need to address the PISA numeracy gap, this app focuses on helping learners improve their confidence and competence in handling mathematical operations involving integers.

Aligned with the MATATAG Curriculum and the Enhanced K–10 Curriculum, it ensures that every learning experience is engaging, inclusive, and reflective of the Department of Education’s standards.

🌟 Recognizing that many learners face challenges with numeracy—especially in areas with limited internet access—MaTHech was designed as an offline educational tool to promote inclusivity and equal learning opportunities for all students.

🎯 Made Just for You!

Tailored for Grade 7 students, this application aims to enhance skills in integer operations, preparing learners for higher-level mathematical concepts. Through hands-on, fun activities, students will gain both confidence and joy in learning mathematics.
