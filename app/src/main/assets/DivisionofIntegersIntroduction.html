Division of Integers

Division of integers involves the grouping of items. It includes both positive numbers and negative numbers. Just like multiplication, the division of integers follows specific sign rules that determine whether your answer will be positive or negative.

📋 Three Cases of Integer Division

➕➗➕
Positive ÷ Positive
Dividing two positive numbers always gives a positive result
➖➗➖
Negative ÷ Negative
Dividing two negative numbers always gives a positive result
➕➗➖
Mixed Signs
Dividing numbers with different signs always gives a negative result
💡 Quick Tip: The sign rules for division are the same as multiplication! Same signs = positive result, different signs = negative result.

Sign Rule Quick Reference

+ ÷ + = +
Both positive → Positive
− ÷ − = +
Both negative → Positive
+ ÷ − = −
Mixed signs → Negative
− ÷ + = −
Mixed signs → Negative
