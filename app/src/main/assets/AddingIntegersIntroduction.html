Adding Integers

Adding integers is the process of finding the sum of two or more integers. It may result in an increase or a decrease in value, depending on whether the integers are positive, negative, or a mixture.

📚 Rules for Adding Integers

Rule 1: Additive Inverse The sum of an integer and its additive inverse is 0.
2 + (−2) = 0
Rule 2: Positive + Positive Adding two positive integers always results in a positive value.
3 + 3 = 6
Rule 3: Negative + Negative Adding two negative integers always results in a negative number.
−5 + (−5) = −10
Rule 4: Adding Zero Adding integers with 0 results in the same number.
4 + 0 = 4, −2 + 0 = −2
Rule 5: Different Signs If the signs are different, subtract and take the sign of the number with the larger value.
−7 + 3 = −4
